# Baobab Guide Creation Template

## How to Create a High-Quality Guide for Baobab

This template helps you write guides that are SEO-optimized, user-friendly, and accurate.

---

## Part 1: Planning Your Guide

### Step 1: Topic Research
- [ ] Search Google for similar guides
- [ ] Check what questions users ask (Google "People Also Ask")
- [ ] Identify the primary keyword (e.g., "how to apply for passport")
- [ ] Identify secondary keywords (e.g., "Nigerian passport", "passport application")
- [ ] List any related guides on Baobab

### Step 2: SME (Subject Matter Expert) Input
- [ ] Identify an expert in this domain
- [ ] Interview them for current information
- [ ] Get permission to cite their knowledge
- [ ] Verify costs/timelines/procedures are current

### Step 3: Official Source Gathering
- [ ] Government website (CAC, FIRS, NIS, etc.)
- [ ] Official forms and procedures
- [ ] Current regulations and laws
- [ ] Contact information for relevant agencies

## Part 2: Structure Your Guide

Use the **12-Section Template** from baobab-02-page-production-template.md:

### Section 1: Quick Answer (50-100 words)
**Purpose**: Immediate answer for impatient readers
**Tone**: Direct, conversational
**Include**: 
- How long it takes
- How much it costs
- Key requirement

**Example**:
> "Nigerian passport renewal costs ₦50,000 (standard) or ₦75,000 (expedited). Processing takes 2-4 weeks standard or 5 business days expedited. You'll need your current passport, completed application form, and proof of residence."

### Section 2: Overview (150-200 words)
**Purpose**: Context and importance
**Tone**: Informative
**Include**:
- Why this matters
- Who needs this
- General process overview

**Example**:
> "A Nigerian passport is your primary travel document. It's required for international travel and proves your Nigerian citizenship abroad. Whether you're traveling for tourism, business, or migration, you'll need a valid passport. Renewal is different from replacement—renewal extends your current passport's validity, while replacement is needed if your passport is lost, stolen, or damaged beyond repair."

### Section 3: Definitions (150-200 words)
**Purpose**: Explain key terms
**Tone**: Educational
**Include**:
- Define main concept
- Clarify common misconceptions
- Explain related terms

**Example**:
> "A passport renewal is the process of extending the validity of your current Nigerian passport. Unlike a replacement, which requires repeating the entire original application process, renewal is faster and simpler. Your passport is a travel document issued by the Nigerian Immigration Service that allows you to enter and exit Nigeria and travel internationally. A valid passport must have at least 6 months validity from your departure date for most countries."

### Section 4: Requirements (5-12 bullet points)
**Purpose**: What user needs to complete this task
**Format**: Bullet list
**Include**:
- Documents needed
- Eligibility criteria
- Prerequisites

**Example Requirements Checklist**:
- [ ] Valid current Nigerian passport
- [ ] Completed application form (NIS 1)
- [ ] Birth certificate or National ID
- [ ] Proof of residence (utility bill, rental agreement)
- [ ] Two recent passport-sized photographs (4x6cm)
- [ ] Proof of payment of renewal fee
- [ ] Evidence of name change (if applicable)

### Section 5: Timeline & Steps (8-15 numbered steps)
**Purpose**: Step-by-step how-to
**Format**: Numbered list with details
**Include**:
- Each major step
- Substeps if needed
- Estimated time

**Example**:
1. **Visit the NIS website** (www.immigration.gov.ng) - 5 minutes
   - Click "Online Services"
   - Select "Passport Renewal"

2. **Start online renewal process** - 10 minutes
   - Fill out renewal form (NIS 1)
   - Upload digital passport photo
   - Upload scanned documents

3. **Schedule appointment** - 5 minutes
   - Pick nearest NIS office
   - Choose appointment date
   - Confirm via email

4. **Attend appointment** - 30 minutes
   - Arrive 15 minutes early
   - Bring all original documents
   - Attend biometric capture

5. **Make payment** - 5 minutes
   - Pay ₦50,000 (standard) or ₦75,000 (expedited)
   - Keep receipt

6. **Wait for processing** - 2-4 weeks (standard) or 5 days (expedited)
   - Track status online
   - Receive SMS notification when ready

7. **Collect renewed passport** - 5 minutes
   - Visit NIS office
   - Present reference number
   - Receive new passport

### Section 6: The Regulatory Picture (150-250 words)
**Purpose**: Legal/regulatory context
**Tone**: Factual, authoritative
**Include**:
- Who regulates this
- Relevant laws/policies
- Important compliance info
- Penalties for non-compliance

**Example**:
> "Nigerian passport renewals are processed by the Nigerian Immigration Service (NIS) under the Immigration Act. All Nigerian citizens must renew their passports before expiration. The 2024 renewal fee structure is: Standard service (2-4 weeks): ₦50,000, Expedited service (5 business days): ₦75,000. Traveling without a valid passport is illegal and can result in fines up to ₦100,000 or imprisonment. NIS has offices in all 36 states plus FCT."

### Section 7: Cost Breakdown (Structured table)
**Purpose**: Clear cost estimates
**Format**: Table or structured list

**Example**:

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Standard renewal | ₦50,000 | 2-4 weeks processing |
| Expedited renewal | ₦75,000 | 5 business days |
| Priority renewal | ₦100,000 | 2-3 business days |
| Replacement (lost/damaged) | ₦75,000 | Requires new application |
| **Total (standard)** | **₦50,000** | |

**Optional add-ons**:
- Courier delivery: ₦2,000-₦5,000
- Replacement birth certificate: ₦3,000
- NIN registration: ₦4,000

### Section 8: Common Mistakes (5-8 bullet points)
**Purpose**: Help users avoid pitfalls
**Format**: Red flag bullets
**Include**:
- Planning mistakes
- Document errors
- Procedural mistakes
- Timeline issues

**Example**:
- ❌ Not starting renewal before passport expires (can't travel)
- ❌ Submitting unclear copies of supporting documents (delays)
- ❌ Missing deadline for international travel (passport not ready)
- ❌ Forgetting to pay online before appointment (wasted trip)
- ❌ Bringing incomplete documents (rejection, must restart)
- ❌ Not checking for typos in online form (must recorrect)
- ❌ Choosing wrong appointment time (can't reschedule)

### Section 9: What Happens Next (3-5 related actions)
**Purpose**: Show users what comes after
**Format**: Linked list or next steps

**Example**:
Once your passport is renewed:
1. **Plan your trip** - Now you have 10 years of travel validity
2. **Check visa requirements** - Different countries need different visas
3. **Get travel insurance** - Protect your trip
4. **Learn about travel safety** - Register with embassy if traveling far

### Section 10: Related Services Directory (3-5 providers)
**Purpose**: Cross-sell services
**Format**: Provider cards
**Include**:
- Provider name
- Services offered
- Link to directory

**Example**:
> "Need help with your passport application?"
> 
> - **Delta Immigration Consultants** - Lagos/Abuja
>   Visa processing, immigration advice, document preparation
>   [View Directory Listing]
>
> - **National Immigration Services Hub** - Multiple Locations
>   Direct NIS support, expedited processing help
>   [View Directory Listing]

### Section 11: Tools & Resources
**Purpose**: Helpful calculators/forms
**Format**: Tool cards

**Example**:
- **Passport Fee Calculator** - Calculate exact cost for your situation
- **Appointment Scheduler Checklist** - Remember everything for your NIS appointment
- **Document Checklist** - PDF download of required documents

### Section 12: Verified Sources (5-10 sources)
**Purpose**: Build credibility
**Format**: Numbered list with URLs

**Example**:
1. Nigerian Immigration Service - https://www.immigration.gov.ng
2. NIS Passport Services - https://online.immigration.gov.ng
3. Immigration Act, 2015 - [official PDF]
4. NIS Fee Schedule 2024 - [official document]
5. Interview with Adaobi Okechukwu, NIS Officer - [conducted Jan 2024]

## Part 3: Writing Quality Standards

### Content Quality Checklist
- [ ] **Length**: 1,000-1,500+ words (minimum 800)
- [ ] **Clarity**: Explain like explaining to a friend
- [ ] **Accuracy**: Verify every fact against official sources
- [ ] **Currentness**: All info updated for 2024
- [ ] **Completeness**: Answers all "how to" questions
- [ ] **Actionability**: User can complete task after reading
- [ ] **Tone**: Conversational but professional
- [ ] **Structure**: Follows 12-section template

### SEO Quality Checklist
- [ ] **Title**: <60 characters, includes main keyword
- [ ] **Description**: 120-160 characters, compelling
- [ ] **Keywords**: 4-6 main keywords identified
- [ ] **Headings**: H1 (title), H2 (sections), H3 (subsections)
- [ ] **Links**: 3-5 internal links to related guides
- [ ] **Images**: Include domain-specific icons/diagrams
- [ ] **Alt text**: Descriptive for all images (<125 characters)
- [ ] **Reading time**: ~5-7 minutes at normal pace

### Accuracy Checklist
- [ ] Verified against official government/institutional sources
- [ ] All prices checked for 2024
- [ ] All procedures current and accurate
- [ ] All timelines realistic
- [ ] Legal requirements confirmed
- [ ] Expert review completed
- [ ] SME sign-off received
- [ ] No outdated procedures included

## Common Guide Types

### Type 1: Application/Registration Guides
**Structure**: Requirements → Timeline → Common Mistakes
**Examples**: Passport, CAC registration, TIN, VAT

### Type 2: How-To/Procedure Guides
**Structure**: Overview → Steps → Timeline → Tips
**Examples**: Open bank account, start business, write business plan

### Type 3: Comparison Guides
**Structure**: Overview → Comparison Table → When to Choose → Sources
**Examples**: LLC vs Partnership, Work visa vs Tourist visa

### Type 4: Financial Guides
**Structure**: Overview → Costs → Breakdown → Alternatives → Sources
**Examples**: Business loan costs, fees, insurance premiums

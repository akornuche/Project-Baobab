# How to Register for JAMB UTME

## Quick Answer

Registering for JAMB UTME costs **₦5,000** (examination fee) + **₦1,000** (service charge) = **₦6,000 total** and takes **1-2 weeks** for processing.

## Overview

The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) is the entrance examination for Nigerian universities. Registration is the first step to gaining admission to Nigerian tertiary institutions.

**What you need:**
- Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- Valid email address
- Phone number
- O'Level results (WAEC/NECO)
- Passport photograph

**Estimated Timeline:** 1-2 weeks (processing)  
**Estimated Cost:** ₦6,000

## Understanding the Basics

### What is JAMB UTME?
The JAMB UTME is a computer-based examination that assesses candidates' knowledge in four subjects relevant to their desired course of study. It's the primary criterion for university admission in Nigeria.

### Why You Need JAMB UTME
- Required for university admission
- Determines your eligibility for admission
- Used by all federal and state universities
- Required for NYSC eligibility

### JAMB Scores
- **Maximum Score:** 400
- **Minimum Score:** 160 (varies by institution)
- **Pass Score:** 180-200 (recommended)

## Requirements Checklist

### Mandatory Documents
- ✅ Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- ✅ Valid email address
- ✅ Phone number
- ✅ O'Level results (WAEC/NECO/NABTEB)
- ✅ Passport photograph (white background)
- ✅ JAMB registration PIN (purchased online)

## Timeline & Steps

### Step 1: Purchase Registration PIN (Immediate)
- Visit JAMB website or accredited agent
- Purchase registration PIN (₦5,500 for UTME)
- Receive PIN via SMS/email

### Step 2: Online Registration (30-60 minutes)
- Visit JAMB e-Facility portal
- Create account and log in
- Fill out registration form
- Upload passport photograph
- Select subjects and institutions

### Step 3: Print Registration Slip (Immediate)
- Print registration slip
- Note examination center and date
- Prepare for examination

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
JAMB registration is governed by:
- **JAMB Act Cap J1 LFN 2004**
- **JAMB (Examination) Regulations**
- **Federal Ministry of Education Guidelines**

### Application Authority
- **Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB)** - Primary authority
- **Accredited Agents** - For PIN sales and registration assistance

### Recent Changes
- Fully online registration (2023)
- NIN requirement (2023)
- Biometric verification (2022)

### Cost Breakdown
| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| JAMB UTME Registration | ₦5,000 | Examination fee |
| Service Charge | ₦1,000 | Processing fee |
| **Total** | **₦6,000** | Required payment |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using wrong NIN** - Ensure NIN is active and valid

2. **Not verifying O'Level results** - Ensure all results are available

3. **Poor passport photograph** - Must be recent with white background

4. **Missing deadline** - Register early to avoid last-minute issues

5. **Not reading instructions** - Follow all registration guidelines

## What Happens Next

After JAMB registration, you'll need to:

1. **Sit for the examination** - On the scheduled date
2. **Check results** - Online after 2-3 weeks
3. **Apply for post-UTME** - At your chosen institution
4. **Accept admission** - Through JAMB CAPS

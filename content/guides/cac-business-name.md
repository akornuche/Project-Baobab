# How to Register a Business Name with CAC

## Quick Answer

Registering a Business Name with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) costs **₦50,000** (including VAT) and takes **3-7 days** for standard processing. Using an accredited agent typically adds **₦50,000** in professional fees.

## Overview

Registering your business name with CAC is the first legal step in formalizing your business in Nigeria. This registration gives your business a unique legal identity and allows you to operate under that name.

**What you need:**
- Proposed business name (with alternatives)
- Business address
- Details of owners/members
- NIN (National Identity Number)

## Understanding the Basics

### What is CAC?
The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) is the regulatory body responsible for the registration of companies and businesses in Nigeria. It was established by the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020.

### What is a Business Name?
A Business Name is the simplest form of business structure in Nigeria. It's typically used by sole proprietors or partnerships. Unlike a Limited Company, a Business Name does not provide limited liability protection.

### Why Register?
- Legal recognition of your business name
- Ability to open a business bank account
- Eligibility for government contracts and grants
- Professional credibility with customers and partners

## Requirements Checklist

- ✅ Business name (with 2-3 alternatives)
- ✅ Business address (physical address, not P.O. Box)
- ✅ NIN (National Identity Number) for all owners
- ✅ Phone number and email address
- ✅ Passport photograph of owners
- ✅ Proof of payment of registration fee

## Timeline & Steps

1. **Name Search & Reservation** (1-2 days)
   - Submit proposed names through CAC portal
   - Wait for name approval

2. **Completion of Form A** (Same day)
   - Fill out business registration form
   - Provide details of owners and business

3. **Payment of Fees** (Same day)
   - Pay ₦50,000 registration fee (including VAT)

4. **Submission of Documents** (Same day)
   - Upload completed forms and supporting documents
   - Submit through CAC portal

5. **Certificate of Registration** (3-7 days)
   - CAC processes application
   - Certificate issued and delivered

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
Business Name registration is governed by:
- **Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020**
- **Companies Registration Regulations 2021**

### Legal Obligations
After registration, you must:
- Renew registration annually
- File annual returns with CAC
- Keep proper business records
- Pay applicable taxes (TIN registration required)

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using the wrong form** - Business Name registration uses Form A, not Form C (for Limited Companies)

2. **Ignoring name search** - Always search for name availability before submission to avoid rejection

3. **Not having alternatives** - Submit 2-3 name alternatives to increase approval chances

4. **Using P.O. Box as address** - CAC requires a physical business address

5. **Forgetting NIN** - NIN is now mandatory for all business registration applications

## What Happens Next

After registering your Business Name, you'll need to:

1. **Get your TIN** - Required for all business operations
2. **Open a business bank account** - Essential for business transactions
3. **Register for VAT** - If your sales exceed ₦25 million annually
4. **Register for PAYE** - If you have employees

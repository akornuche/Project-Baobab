# How to Register for Value Added Tax (VAT)

## Quick Answer

Registering for Value Added Tax (VAT) costs **₦0 (free)** and takes **1-2 weeks** through the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) portal. VAT registration is mandatory for businesses with annual turnover exceeding ₦25 million.

## Overview

Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax charged at 7.5% on goods and services in Nigeria. Businesses with annual turnover exceeding ₦25 million must register for VAT with FIRS.

**What you need:**
- Valid TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number)
- Valid email address
- Business registration details
- Bank account information

**Estimated Timeline:** 1-2 weeks (processing)  
**Estimated Cost:** Free

## Understanding the Basics

### What is VAT?
Value Added Tax (VAT) is a consumption tax charged at 7.5% on the sale of goods and services in Nigeria. It's collected by businesses and remitted to FIRS.

### Why You Need VAT Registration
- Mandatory for businesses with turnover > ₦25 million
- Required for government contracts
- Needed for business banking
- Demonstrates tax compliance

### VAT Rates
- **Standard Rate:** 7.5%
- **Zero Rate:** 0% (for certain exports)
- **Exempt:** Some goods and services (education, healthcare)

## Common Mistakes

1. **Missing the ₦25 million threshold** - Register if you exceed this threshold

2. **Using incorrect TIN** - Ensure TIN is active and valid

3. **Not filing returns** - Even with no transactions, file nil returns

4. **Not issuing VAT invoices** - Always issue VAT-compliant invoices

5. **Not remitting on time** - VAT must be remitted by the 21st of each month

## What Happens Next

After VAT registration, you must:

1. **Issue VAT invoices** - For all taxable supplies
2. **File VAT returns** - Monthly by the 21st
3. **Remit VAT** - To FIRS on time
4. **Maintain records** - For audit purposes

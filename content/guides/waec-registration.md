# How to Register for WAEC Examination

## Quick Answer

Registering for WAEC examination costs **₦13,750** (examination fee) + **₦500** (service charge) = **₦14,250 total** and takes **2-3 weeks** for processing.

## Overview

The West African Examinations Council (WAEC) examination is the standard test for secondary school students in Nigeria. Registration is required to sit for the Senior Secondary School Certificate Examination (SSCE).

**What you need:**
- Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- Valid email address
- Phone number
- School information
- Passport photograph

**Estimated Timeline:** 2-3 weeks (processing)  
**Estimated Cost:** ₦14,250

## Understanding the Basics

### What is WAEC SSCE?
The Senior Secondary School Certificate Examination (SSCE) is conducted by WAEC to assess students' academic performance at the end of senior secondary school. It's required for university admission and employment.

### Why You Need WAEC SSCE
- Required for university admission
- Required for employment
- Required for professional certifications
- Proof of academic qualification

### WAEC Exams
- **SSCE (Internal)** - For school candidates
- **SSCE (External)** - For private candidates
- **BECE** - For junior secondary students
- **GCE** - For adult learners

## Requirements Checklist

### Mandatory Documents
- ✅ Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- ✅ Valid email address
- ✅ Phone number
- ✅ School name and code (for school candidates)
- ✅ Passport photograph (white background)
- ✅ WAEC registration Voucher (purchased online)

## Timeline & Steps

### Step 1: Purchase Registration Voucher (Immediate)
- Visit WAEC website or accredited agent
- Purchase registration voucher
- Receive voucher code via SMS/email

### Step 2: Online Registration (30-60 minutes)
- Visit WAEC online registration portal
- Create account and log in
- Fill out registration form
- Upload passport photograph
- Select subjects (minimum 8, maximum 9)

### Step 3: Print Registration Slip (Immediate)
- Print registration slip
- Note examination center and date
- Prepare for examination

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
WAEC registration is governed by:
- **West African Examinations Council Act**
- **Federal Ministry of Education Guidelines**
- **WAEC Regulations**

### Application Authority
- **West African Examinations Council (WAEC)** - Primary authority
- **Accredited Agents** - For voucher sales and registration assistance

### Recent Changes
- Fully online registration (2023)
- NIN requirement (2023)
- Biometric verification (2022)

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using wrong NIN** - Ensure NIN is active and valid

2. **Not selecting subjects early** - Choose subjects before registration closes

3. **Poor passport photograph** - Must be recent with white background

4. **Missing deadline** - Register early to avoid last-minute issues

5. **Not verifying registration** - Always verify registration details

## What Happens Next

After WAEC registration, you'll need to:

1. **Sit for the examination** - On the scheduled dates
2. **Check results** - Online after 2-3 months
3. **Apply for post-UTME** - If applying to universities
4. **Retake if necessary** - For subjects you failed

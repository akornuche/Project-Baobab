# How to Register for Your Voter Card

## Quick Answer

Registering for a Voter Card is **free** and takes **10-20 minutes** at any Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) registration center nationwide.

## Overview

The Voter Card (Permanent Voter's Card or PVC) is your official identification for voting in Nigerian elections. Registration is required to participate in federal, state, and local government elections.

**What you need:**
- Valid means of identification
- Phone number
- Biometric data (fingerprints, facial photo)
- NIN (National Identity Number)

**Estimated Timeline:** 10-20 minutes (enrollment)  
**Estimated Cost:** Free

## Understanding the Basics

### What is a Voter Card?
The Permanent Voter's Card (PVC) is an official identification card issued by INEC that allows you to vote in Nigerian elections. It contains your biometric data and voter information.

### Why You Need a Voter Card
- Required to vote in elections
- Official government identification
- Required for some government services
- Proof of residency

### What is BVAS?
BVAS (Biometric Verification Application System) is the system used to capture your biometric data during voter registration.

## Requirements Checklist

### Mandatory Documents (One of the following)
- ✅ Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- ✅ Birth certificate
- ✅ Voter card from previous registration
- ✅ Old passport
- ✅ Driver's license

### Additional Requirements
- ✅ Valid phone number
- ✅ Email address (optional)
- ✅ Photograph

## Timeline & Steps

### Step 1: Find Registration Center
- Locate nearest INEC registration center
- Check INEC website for center locations

### Step 2: Enrollment Visit (10-20 minutes)
- Visit INEC registration center
- Fill out registration form
- Provide biometric data (fingerprints, facial photo)
- Submit documents for verification

### Step 3: Receive Registration Slip (Immediate)
- Receive registration slip with transaction ID
- Note your transaction ID for PVC tracking

### Step 4: Collect PVC (1-2 weeks)
- Wait for PVC to be printed
- Collect from designated pickup center
- Verify PVC information is correct

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
Voter registration is governed by:
- **Electoral Act 2022**
- **INEC (Registration of Voters) Regulations 2022**

### Application Authority
- **Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC)** - Primary authority
- **Registration Centers** - Authorized agents across Nigeria

### Recent Changes
- NIN requirement for registration (2023)
- Digital voter registration system (2022)
- Early voting options (2023)

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using expired identification** - Documents must be valid

2. **Missing NIN requirement** - NIN is mandatory for all new registrations

3. **Not verifying biometric capture** - Ensure all fingerprints are captured

4. **Not collecting PVC** - PVC is not mailed automatically - must collect

5. **Using unverified centers** - Only use official INEC registration centers

## What Happens Next

After obtaining your Voter Card, remember to:

1. **Verify PVC information** - Check all details are correct
2. **Find your polling unit** - Use INEC portal to locate polling station
3. **Prepare for election day** - Bring PVC and valid ID

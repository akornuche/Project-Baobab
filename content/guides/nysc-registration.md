# How to Register for NYSC Service

## Quick Answer

Registering for NYSC service costs **₦0 (free)** and takes **2-4 weeks** for the entire registration and mobilization process.

## Overview

The National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) is a mandatory one-year service program for all Nigerian graduates. Registration is the first step to completing your national service obligation.

**What you need:**
- Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- Valid email address
- Phone number
- Degree certificate or result slip
- Medical fitness certificate

**Estimated Timeline:** 2-4 weeks (registration to mobilization)  
**Estimated Cost:** Free

## Understanding the Basics

### What is NYSC?
The National Youth Service Corps is a mandatory one-year service program for all Nigerian graduates. It aims to foster national unity and development by deploying graduates to different parts of the country.

### Why You Need NYSC
- Required for employment in Nigeria
- Required for professional certifications
- Required for government jobs
- Part of national service obligation

### NYSC Streams
1. **Full-Time Degree Holders** - Regular graduates
2. **Direct Entry** - NCE, OND, HND holders
3. **Mobilization** - For overseas graduates

## Requirements Checklist

### Mandatory Documents
- ✅ Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- ✅ Valid email address
- ✅ Phone number
- ✅ Degree certificate or result slip
- ✅ Medical fitness certificate
- ✅ Passport photograph

### Additional Requirements
- ✅ Birth certificate or age declaration
- ✅ Local government area of origin
- ✅ Next of kin information

## Timeline & Steps

### Week 1: Online Registration
- Day 1-2: Create NYSC portal account
- Day 3-4: Fill out registration form
- Day 5: Upload required documents

### Week 2-3: Stream Allocation
- Day 1-3: Wait for stream allocation
- Day 4-5: Print call-up letter

### Week 4: Mobilization
- Day 1-3: Attend orientation camp
- Day 4-5: Receive posting letter

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
NYSC registration is governed by:
- **NYSC Act Cap N137 LFN 2004**
- **NYSC Regulations**
- **Federal Ministry of Youth Development Guidelines**

### Application Authority
- **National Youth Service Corps (NYSC)** - Primary authority
- **State NYSC Offices** - Local implementation

### Recent Changes
- Fully online registration (2023)
- NIN requirement (2023)
- Digital call-up letters (2022)

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using wrong NIN** - Ensure NIN is active and valid

2. **Missing registration deadline** - Register early

3. **Poor medical certificate** - Must be from accredited hospital

4. **Not verifying registration** - Always verify registration details

5. **Missing orientation camp** - Attending camp is mandatory

## What Happens Next

After NYSC registration, you'll need to:

1. **Attend orientation camp** - Mandatory 3-week camp
2. **Serve at assigned location** - One-year service
3. **Complete service requirements** - Monthly reports
4. **Receive PPA** - Passout certificate

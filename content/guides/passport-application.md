# How to Apply for a Nigerian Passport

## Quick Answer

Applying for a Nigerian passport costs **₦25,000 for standard (32-page)** or **₦45,000 for premium (64-page)** and takes **4-6 weeks for standard processing** or **1-2 weeks for expedited**.

## Overview

A Nigerian passport is your official travel document that verifies your citizenship and allows you to travel internationally. The application process has been digitized through the Nigerian Immigration Service (NIS) portal, making it more accessible and efficient.

**What you need:**
- Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- Valid email address
- Phone number
- Recent passport photograph
- Proof of payment

**Estimated Timeline:** 4-6 weeks (standard) or 1-2 weeks (expedited)  
**Estimated Cost:** ₦25,000-45,000

## Understanding the Basics

### What is a Nigerian Passport?
A Nigerian passport is an official travel document issued by the Nigerian Immigration Service (NIS) that verifies your Nigerian citizenship and allows you to travel internationally.

### Types of Nigerian Passports
1. **Standard Passport (32 pages)** - Regular travel passport
2. **Premium Passport (64 pages)** - Extended validity for frequent travelers
3. **Diplomatic Passport** - For government officials
4. **Official Passport** - For government employees on official duty

### Why You Need a Passport
- International travel
- Visa applications
- Proof of citizenship
- Travel documentation for immigration

## Requirements Checklist

### Mandatory Documents
- ✅ Valid NIN (National Identity Number)
- ✅ Valid email address
- ✅ Phone number
- ✅ Recent passport photograph (white background)
- ✅ Proof of payment for passport application
- ✅ Birth certificate or age declaration

### Additional Requirements for Minors
- ✅ Parental consent
- ✅ Parents' passports (copies)
- ✅ Birth certificate

## Timeline & Steps

### Week 1: Application Preparation
- Day 1-2: Gather required documents and prepare passport photograph
- Day 3-4: Create account on Nigerian Immigration Service portal
- Day 5: Fill out application form online

### Week 2: Application Submission
- Day 1-2: Upload required documents and photographs
- Day 3: Schedule biometrics appointment
- Day 4-5: Pay application fees

### Week 3-4: Biometrics & Processing
- Day 1-2: Attend biometrics appointment at NIS office
- Day 3-7: Application processing by NIS

### Week 5-6: Passport Collection
- Day 1-3: Receive notification when passport is ready
- Day 4-5: Collect passport from NIS office or authorized center

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
Passport application is governed by:
- **Nigerian Immigration Act**
- **Nigerian Immigration Service Act 2015**

### Application Authority
- **Nigerian Immigration Service (NIS)** - Primary authority for passport issuance
- **Federal Ministry of Interior** - Oversight body

### Recent Changes
- Online application portal implemented (2023)
- Biometrics requirements strengthened (2022)
- Expedited processing option added (2022)

## Common Mistakes

1. **Using old passport photographs** - Must be recent (within 6 months) with white background

2. **Not having NIN ready** - NIN is mandatory for all passport applications

3. **Filling incorrect information** - Double-check all fields before submission

4. **Missing biometrics appointment** - Failure to attend will delay processing

5. **Using unofficial portals** - Only use the official NIS portal (immigration.gov.ng)

## What Happens Next

After applying for your passport, you'll need to:

1. **Track your application** - Use the NIS tracking portal
2. **Prepare for biometrics** - Bring original documents to appointment
3. **Collect your passport** - Bring ID when collecting

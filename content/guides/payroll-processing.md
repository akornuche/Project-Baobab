# How to Process Payroll in Nigeria

## Quick Answer

Processing payroll in Nigeria costs **₦50,000-150,000 annually** (depending on payroll service provider) and takes **1-3 hours per pay period** for in-house processing.

## Overview

Payroll processing involves calculating employee salaries, deducting taxes and statutory contributions, and ensuring timely payment. It's a critical function for any business with employees.

**What you need:**
- Valid TIN for business
- Valid NIN for all employees
- Bank details for salary disbursement
- Employee contracts
- Payroll software or system

**Estimated Timeline:** 1-3 hours (per pay period)  
**Estimated Cost:** ₦50,000-150,000 (annually)

## Understanding the Basics

### What is Payroll Processing?
Payroll processing is the system of calculating and distributing employee salaries, including deductions for taxes, pension, and other statutory contributions. It ensures employees are paid accurately and on time.

### Why Proper Payroll is Important
- Legal compliance (PAYE, Pension, NHF, HIT)
- Employee satisfaction
- Accurate financial records
- Tax compliance
- Audit readiness

### Payroll Components
1. **Gross Salary** - Total salary before deductions
2. **Deductions** - PAYE, Pension, NHF, HIT
3. **Net Salary** - Take-home pay after deductions
4. **Employer Contributions** - Pension, NHF, HIT

## Requirements Checklist

### Mandatory Requirements
- ✅ Valid TIN for business
- ✅ Valid NIN for all employees
- ✅ Employee contracts
- ✅ Bank details for salary disbursement
- ✅ Payroll register or software
- ✅ Tax compliance certificate

### Additional Requirements
- ✅ Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) details
- ✅ Health Insurance Provider details
- ✅ Payroll payment schedule

## Timeline & Steps

### Monthly Payroll Processing
- Week 1: Collect attendance and performance data
- Week 2: Calculate salaries and deductions
- Week 3: Prepare payment file
- Week 4: Disburse salaries and file tax returns

### Annual Payroll Tasks
- January: Prepare year-end reports
- Q1: File tax returns
- Q2: Renew tax compliance certificate
- Q3-Q4: Continue monthly processing

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
Payroll processing is governed by:
- **Personal Income Tax Act**
- **Pension Act 2014**
- **NHF Act**
- **HIT Act**

### Application Authority

## Common Mistakes

1. **Missing statutory deductions** - Ensure all required deductions are made

2. **Late tax payments** - PAYE must be remitted by the 21st of each month

3. **Incorrect NIN** - All employees must have valid NIN

4. **Not keeping records** - Maintain payroll records for 6 years

5. **Manual calculations** - Use software to avoid errors

## What Happens Next

After setting up payroll, you'll need to:

1. **Process first payroll** - Calculate and disburse salaries
2. **File tax returns** - Monthly PAYE returns
3. **Issue payslips** - To all employees
4. **Maintain records** - For audit and compliance

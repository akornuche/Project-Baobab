# How to Apply for a Business Loan in Nigeria

## Quick Answer

Applying for a business loan costs **0-2% of loan amount** (processing fees) and takes **2-8 weeks** for approval and disbursement.

## Overview

Business loans provide capital for starting, growing, or expanding a business. Nigerian banks, microfinance institutions, and online lenders offer various loan products for businesses of all sizes.

**What you need:**
- Valid TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number)
- CAC registration certificate
- Business bank account
- Financial statements
- Business plan

**Estimated Timeline:** 2-8 weeks (approval process)  
**Estimated Cost:** 0-2% of loan amount

## Understanding the Basics

### What is a Business Loan?
A business loan is a sum of money borrowed from a financial institution to fund business activities such as startup costs, inventory purchase, equipment acquisition, or business expansion.

### Types of Business Loans
1. **Term Loan** - Fixed amount with fixed repayment schedule
2. **Overdraft** - Flexible borrowing up to credit limit
3. **Line of Credit** - Revolving credit facility
4. **Working Capital Loan** - For day-to-day operations
5. **Equipment Loan** - For purchasing equipment

### Why You Need a Business Loan
- Startup capital
- Business expansion
- Inventory purchase
- Equipment acquisition
- Working capital

## Requirements Checklist

### Mandatory Documents
- ✅ Valid TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number)
- ✅ CAC registration certificate
- ✅ Business bank account details
- ✅ Financial statements (last 2 years)
- ✅ Business plan
- ✅ Valid NIN for all directors

### Additional Requirements
- ✅ Collateral documentation (for secured loans)
- ✅ Personal financial statements
- ✅ References

## Timeline & Steps

### Week 1-2: Preparation
- Day 1-3: Gather required documents
- Day 4-5: Prepare business plan and financial projections

### Week 3-4: Application
- Day 1-2: Submit application to lender
- Day 3-4: Wait for initial review
- Day 5: Provide additional information if requested

### Week 5-8: Approval & Disbursement
- Week 3-4: Credit assessment
- Week 5-6: Collateral valuation (if applicable)
- Week 7-8: Loan approval and disbursement

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
Business loans are governed by:
- **Banks and Other Financial Institutions Act**
- **Central Bank of Nigeria Guidelines**
- **Credit Reporting Act**

### Application Authority
- **Banks** - Commercial banks
- **Microfinance Banks** - For small businesses
- **Online Lenders** - Fintech lenders
- **Development Finance Institutions** - Long-term financing

### Recent Changes

- Automated credit scoring (2022)

### Cost Breakdown
| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Application Fee | ₦0-5,000 | Varies by lender |
| Processing Fee | 0-2% of loan | As per agreement |
| Interest Rate | 12-30% p.a. | As per risk profile |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Missing TIN requirement** - TIN is mandatory for all business loans

2. **Incomplete documentation** - Ensure all documents are provided

3. **Unrealistic business plan** - Ensure projections are realistic

4. **Not shopping around** - Compare different lenders

5. **Ignoring repayment capacity** - Only borrow what you can repay

## What Happens Next

After applying for a business loan, you'll need to:

1. **Maintain regular communication** - With lender
2. **Prepare for site visit** - If required
3. **Negotiate terms** - If necessary
4. **Sign loan agreement** - Once approved

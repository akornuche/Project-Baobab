# How to Register a Trademark in Nigeria

## Quick Answer

Registering a trademark in Nigeria costs **₦45,000-75,000** (government and professional fees) and takes **6-12 months** for the entire registration process.

## Overview

Registering a trademark protects your business name, logo, or slogan from unauthorized use. It's essential for building brand identity and preventing competitors from using your branding.

**What you need:**
- Unique trademark (name, logo, or slogan)
- Business registration details
- Valid NIN for applicant
- Trademark description
- Goods/services classification

**Estimated Timeline:** 6-12 months (full registration)  
**Estimated Cost:** ₦45,000-75,000

## Understanding the Basics

### What is a Trademark?
A trademark is a recognizable sign, design, or expression that identifies products or services from a particular source. It can be a name, logo, symbol, or slogan.

### Why You Need a Trademark
- Protects your brand identity
- Prevents competitors from using your branding
- Adds value to your business
- Required for business expansion
- Legal protection against infringement

### Types of Trademarks
1. **Word Mark** - Business name or slogan
2. **Logo Mark** - Design or symbol
3. **Combination Mark** - Word + logo
4. **Service Mark** - For services instead of products

## Requirements Checklist

### Mandatory Documents
- ✅ Unique trademark (name, logo, or slogan)
- ✅ Business registration certificate (CAC)
- ✅ Valid NIN for applicant
- ✅ Trademark description
- ✅ Goods/services classification
- ✅ Passport photograph of applicant

### Additional Requirements
- ✅ Power of attorney (if using agent)
- ✅ Priority document (if claiming priority)
- ✅ Trademark specimen (if applicable)

## Timeline & Steps

### Month 1: Application
- Week 1: Trademark search and selection
- Week 2: Prepare application documents
- Week 3: Submit application to trademark office
- Week 4: Receive application number

### Months 2-6: Examination
- Month 2-3: Trademark examination
- Month 4-5: Address any objections
- Month 6: Publication for opposition

### Months 7-12: Registration
- Month 7-8: Wait for opposition period
- Month 9: Receive registration certificate
- Month 10-12: Final approval and issuance

## The Regulatory Picture

### Governing Law
Trademark registration is governed by:
- **Trademarks Act Cap T13 LFN 2004**
- **Trademarks Regulations**
- **Nigerian Intellectual Property Office Guidelines**

### Application Authority
- **Trademarks Registry (under Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment)**
- **Nigerian Intellectual Property Office (NIPCO)**

### Recent Changes
- Online application system (2023)
- Reduced processing time (2022)
- Digital certificates (2023)

### Cost Breakdown
| Fee | Amount | Notes |

| Government Fee | ₦35,000 | Registration fee |
| Professional Fee | ₦10,000-30,000 | Agent/attorney fee |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Not conducting trademark search** - Verify uniqueness before applying

2. **Using descriptive names** - Trademarks must be distinctive

3. **Missing deadline** - Response to objections must be timely

4. **Not protecting internationally** - Consider international registration for exports

5. **Not renewing on time** - Trademarks must be renewed every 7 years

## What Happens Next

After registering your trademark, you'll need to:

1. **Use the trademark** - Start using it in business
2. **Monitor for infringement** - Watch for unauthorized use
3. **Renew on time** - Every 7 years
4. **Licensing** - Consider licensing if expanding

## Content Production Workflow

### Phases

#### Phase 1: Planning (Days 1-2 per guide)
- Research current regulations & procedures
- Interview subject matter experts (SMEs)
- Compile official government/institutional documentation
- Create outline with 12-section template

#### Phase 2: Writing (Days 3-5 per guide)
- Write content following baobab-02 template
- 800-1,500+ words per guide
- Include step-by-step instructions
- Add cost breakdowns & timeline estimates
- Include real examples & case studies

#### Phase 3: Review (Days 6-7 per guide)
- Subject matter expert review
- Fact-checking against official sources
- Update with latest 2024 information
- Legal/compliance review (where applicable)

#### Phase 4: Publishing (Day 8 per guide)
- Format for database
- Create metadata
- Add to Meilisearch index
- Schedule publication
- Share on social media

### Timeline

| Week | Guides | Status | Target |
|------|--------|--------|--------|
| Week 1 | 1-20 | PRIORITY 1 | Complete by Friday |
| Week 2-3 | 21-45 | PRIORITY 2 | Complete by end of Week 3 |
| Week 4+ | 46-65+ | PRIORITY 3+ | Ongoing |

### SLAs

- **Research**: 1 day per guide
- **Writing**: 2-3 days per guide
- **Review**: 1-2 days per guide
- **Publishing**: Same day upon approval
- **Total per guide**: 4-7 days
- **Batch efficiency**: 2-3 guides completed simultaneously

## Content Quality Standards

### Minimum Requirements Per Guide

- ✅ 12-section template (from baobab-02-page-production-template.md)
- ✅ 800-1,500+ words (minimum 1,000 recommended)
- ✅ Step-by-step numbered instructions
- ✅ Cost breakdown with 2024 rates
- ✅ Timeline estimate (hours/days/weeks)
- ✅ Common mistakes section (3-5 items)
- ✅ 5+ verified sources with URLs
- ✅ Professional reviewer attribution
- ✅ SEO metadata (title, description, keywords)
- ✅ Related guides cross-linking

### Accuracy Standards

- ✅ Information verified against official sources
- ✅ 2024 pricing and regulations current
- ✅ No outdated procedures
- ✅ Legal accuracy confirmed
- ✅ Compliance with FIRS/CAC/NIS regulations
- ✅ Reviewer sign-off required

### SEO Standards

- ✅ Title: <60 characters, includes main keyword
- ✅ Description: 120-160 characters
- ✅ Keywords: 4-6 relevant terms
- ✅ H1/H2 hierarchy correct
- ✅ Internal links: 3-5 per guide
- ✅ Alt text on all images
- ✅ Reading time: ~5-7 minutes

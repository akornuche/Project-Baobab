## 🔄 Process Workflow

### Content Creation Cycle (Per Guide)
1. **Planning** (1 day)
   - Topic research
   - SME identification
   - Source gathering

2. **Writing** (2-3 days)
   - First draft
   - Section completion
   - Self-review

3. **Review** (2 days)
   - SME review
   - Editorial review
   - Legal review (if needed)

4. **Publishing** (1 day)
   - Database entry
   - Meilisearch indexing
   - Social sharing

**Total per guide**: 4-7 days  
**Batch efficiency**: 2-3 guides simultaneously = 2-3 guides/day

### Timeline Math
- 20 Priority 1 guides: ~10 days (2-3 parallel teams)
- 25 Priority 2 guides: ~12 days
- 20 Priority 3 guides: ~10 days
- **Total**: ~32 days for 65 guides (4 weeks)
